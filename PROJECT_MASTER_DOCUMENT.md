## 3. 🛡️ Agency Operating Rules & Stress-Free Policies

### 1. Domain Ownership Policy:
- Domain charges are **NOT** included in the website design fee.
- Client buys their domain directly on GoDaddy/Hostinger (~₹800–₹900 direct registrar fee) using their own Gmail and payment card.
- **Benefit**: 100% client ownership from Day 1, zero recurring liability or domain renewal headache for the agency. We provide 100% step-by-step guidance on call/WhatsApp to buy it in 2 minutes.

### 2. Hosting & SSL Policy:
- Hosted on **Netlify Free Cloud Hosting** with automatic lifetime free SSL (🔒 security padlock).
- Zero server cost for agency, blazing fast global CDN for clients, and zero recurring monthly hosting fees for the client.

### 3. Step-by-Step Workflow:
1. **Inquiry & Quick Demo**: Client sends basic business details (name, services, phone, photos). Agency creates a **Quick Demo Preview** to lock layout, design, colors, and typography.
2. **50% Advance Commitment**: Client approves the demo, pays 50% advance, and submits all final content in one consolidated batch.
3. **2–3 Days Final Build**: Agency builds the complete final website on a preview/staging link.
4. **1 Consolidated Revision Round**: Client gets 1 round of minor text, contact numbers, and alignment adjustments (no new sections or extra photo dumps).
5. **Full Payment & Live Domain Connection**:
   - **CRITICAL AGENCY RULE**: Client verifies the final website on the preview link.
   - Client clears the remaining 50% balance payment.
   - *Immediately upon payment*, the agency connects the live domain and transfers files/credentials!
6. **7 Days Bug-Fixing Support**: Agency provides 7 days of active bug-fixing support (link checks, form tests, mobile responsiveness). Project is officially signed off.
